## Workflows

Tools for automating repetitive marketing work. Common use cases include content publishing, campaign launches, reporting, alerts, and handoffs between tools.

- [n8n](https://n8n.io) - A workflow automation platform for building AI agents, API integrations, and scheduled marketing jobs with visual nodes and code.
- [Make](https://www.make.com) - A visual automation platform for connecting marketing apps and building multi-step workflows without code.
- [Zapier](https://zapier.com) - An app-to-app automation tool for simple triggers and actions across marketing tools.
- [Clay](https://www.clay.com) - A data enrichment and AI research platform for building audiences, researching accounts, and personalizing campaigns.

[Contribute to list!](#contributing)

## AI

Tools for applying AI to marketing work, including writing, research, analysis, and building agents.

- [Claude](https://claude.ai) - An AI assistant from Anthropic for writing, analysis, research, and agent-style work across documents and tools.
- [Claude Code](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-code) - An agentic coding tool for building marketing systems, scripts, and internal tools from the terminal.
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) - An AI assistant from OpenAI for drafting, analysis, research, and data cleanup.
- [Cursor](https://cursor.com) - An AI code editor for building and iterating on marketing tools and landing pages.

[Contribute to list!](#contributing)

## Research

Tools for pulling public web and social data into your marketing systems, such as competitor pages, trending posts, reviews, and job postings.

- [Apify](https://apify.com) - A web scraping platform with ready-made actors for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, Google Maps, and job boards.
- [Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev) - An API for turning websites into clean Markdown or structured data for AI agents. [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/mendableai/firecrawl?style=social)](https://github.com/mendableai/firecrawl)
- [Exa](https://exa.ai) - A web search API built for AI applications, useful for research agents and finding similar pages.
- [Crawl4AI](https://github.com/unclecode/crawl4ai) - An open-source crawler for turning websites into AI-ready data. [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/unclecode/crawl4ai?style=social)](https://github.com/unclecode/crawl4ai)

[Contribute to list!](#contributing)

## Content

Tools for creating, editing, and repurposing content at volume across social, video, and the web.

- [Descript](https://www.descript.com) - A video and podcast editor that lets you edit by editing the transcript, useful for turning long recordings into clips.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com) - A design tool for social graphics, carousels, thumbnails, and simple video.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) - An open-source command-line tool for cutting, captioning, and reformatting video in code, useful for automated editing pipelines.

[Contribute to list!](#contributing)

## AI Search

Tools for tracking and improving how brands show up in AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and others. Also called AEO or GEO.

- [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com) - An AI search platform for tracking brand visibility in AI answers and building agents that improve it.
- [Peec AI](https://peec.ai) - An AI search visibility tool for tracking how often brands appear and get cited in AI answers.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org) - The structured data vocabulary that makes pages easier for search engines and AI to read.

[Contribute to list!](#contributing)

## SEO

Tools for keyword research, site audits, and search performance.

- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) - Free search performance data straight from Google, including queries, clicks, and indexing issues.
- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) - An SEO suite for keyword research, backlinks, content gaps, and site audits.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) - An SEO and content research suite with competitive analysis and rank tracking.

## Email

Tools for newsletters, lifecycle campaigns, and nurture sequences.

- [Customer.io](https://customer.io) - A messaging platform for behavior-based email, push, and SMS campaigns.
- [Braze](https://www.braze.com) - A customer engagement platform for lifecycle campaigns at scale.
- [Marketo](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo.html) - A B2B marketing automation platform for email campaigns, lead scoring, and nurture programs.
- [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com) - A newsletter platform with growth, referral, and monetization tools.

## Data

Tools for storing, syncing, and activating marketing data so agents and campaigns run on the same source of truth.

- [Supabase](https://supabase.com) - A Postgres database with built-in APIs, useful as the data layer for marketing agents and dashboards.
- [Segment](https://segment.com) - A customer data platform for collecting and routing event data to marketing tools.
- [Hightouch](https://hightouch.com) - A reverse ETL and audience tool for syncing warehouse data into ad platforms and email tools.

[Contribute to list!](#contributing)

## Analytics

Tools for measuring traffic, conversion, attribution, and campaign performance.

- [Google Analytics 4](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/) - Web and app analytics for traffic sources, events, and conversions.
- [PostHog](https://posthog.com) - An open-source product analytics suite with experiments, funnels, and session replay. [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/PostHog/posthog?style=social)](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog)
- [Amplitude](https://amplitude.com) - A product and marketing analytics platform for funnels, retention, and experiments.

## Learning
